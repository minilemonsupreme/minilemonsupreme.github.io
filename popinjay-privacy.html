Popinjay privacy policy

Last updated: 6 November 2026

Popinjay is a small Android app that pops up a reminder when you open apps or websites you chose. This page explains what it uses and what it keeps. In short: everything stays on your phone, and nothing is collected or sent anywhere.

What Popinjay uses, and why

Popinjay uses Android's Accessibility service only to notice when you open one of the apps or websites you chose, so it can show your reminder. With your permission it sees:

  • Which app is open.
  • In web browsers, the web address in the address bar. It looks only for the address. It skips password fields and ignores the rest of the screen, including what you type.

The current web address is held in memory only while you're on that page, and forgotten when you switch apps. It is never saved, logged or shared.

Popinjay asks you to agree before it sends you to turn on the Accessibility setting, and you can switch it off at any time in your phone's Accessibility settings.

What Popinjay keeps on your phone

  • Your reminders: the apps and websites you chose, the message, the button and the pop-up style.
  • When each reminder last popped up for each of its apps or websites, and when you last opened a reminder's button app, so it knows when to rest.
  • Any pictures you add for the pop-up, saved as small images in the app's private storage.
  • A few settings, such as whether Popinjay is asleep and whether you've seen the tour.

This stays in the app's private storage on your phone. It isn't included in Google's cloud backup or device-to-device transfer. If you choose Save a backup, Popinjay writes a file to the place you pick; what happens to that file is up to you.

What Popinjay doesn't do

  • It has no internet permission, so it cannot send anything anywhere.
  • It doesn't collect personal information, and has no accounts, ads, analytics or trackers.
  • It doesn't sell or share any data.

Links

A reminder's button may open another app or a web link you chose. A hidden About page has an optional link to the developer's Ko-fi page. These open in other apps or your browser, which have their own privacy policies.

Deleting your data

Delete reminders in the app, or uninstall Popinjay to remove everything it kept.

Children

Popinjay isn't directed at children and doesn't knowingly collect anything from anyone.

Changes

If this policy changes, the new version will be posted on this page with a new date.
